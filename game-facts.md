# Block Kitz — Game Facts Sheet

Use these facts verbatim or lightly rephrased in marketing copy. They are accurate as of **2026-04-25** for the **v1.0.1 build** now in Google Play Closed Testing.

## 🎮 Game Overview

**Block Kitz** is a casual block-puzzle game for Android (iOS coming later). It's designed to be enjoyed by players of all ages, including children, with a strong focus on kid-safe content, non-personalized ads, and zero behavioural tracking.

**Tagline options:**
- "Place. Pop. Play. Block Kitz — the cozy block puzzle for everyone."
- "15,000+ levels across 30 themed chapters — three distinct modes, infinite daily challenges."
- "Kid-safe. Non-personalized ads only. No profiles built. No tracking."

## ✨ Core Features

### Three Distinct Game Modes

1. **Classic Mode** — An 8×8 grid where you drag & drop block pieces from a 3-piece tray onto the board. Fill full rows or columns to clear them and earn points. Features gems, obstacles, and a chapter-based journey through 10 themed chapters starting at "Rookie Grounds."

2. **Pivot Blocks Mode** — A modern falling-block puzzler with SRS rotation, 7-bag randomiser, and 10 chapters from "Cloudhop" to the "Empyrean Throne." T-Spin bonuses, hold piece, and guideline scoring. Escalating difficulty throughout.

3. **Adventure Mode** — Collect gems, dodge bombs, thaw frozen cells, and unlock locked treasures across 10 mystical chapters. Each chapter has its own rules, rewards, and visual world.

### What's Inside

- **15,000+ levels total across 30 themed chapters** (5,000 per mode × 3 modes; 10 chapters per mode). Use this verbatim; do not quote per-mode counts separately in public copy.
- **30 beautifully-themed chapter backgrounds**
- **Daily rewards** — 7-day login streak calendar with escalating prizes
- **Daily Challenge** — a fresh puzzle every day with special modifiers (Precision, Speed, Gem Rush, Ice Fury). Effectively infinite daily challenges over time.
- **Spin wheel** — earn free spins, win coins and power-ups
- **Power-ups** — bomb, row-blast, column-blast, undo, streak shield
- **Achievements** — 22 hidden achievements + session-based mastery badges
- **Streaks & combos** — clear multiple lines for combo bonuses (up to 5x ULTRA COMBO)
- **Beautiful music** — 4 ambient piano moods (Peaceful Garden, Rainy Evening, Ocean Calm, Starlit Night)

## 🛡️ Kid-Safe Positioning

- **Minimal data.** Only the device's advertising ID, and only when the player deliberately taps "Watch Ad" — used to serve a non-personalized ad. No profiles. No behavioural tracking. No data sharing. **Do not claim "zero data collection"** — it contradicts the Data Safety form.
- **No forced ads.** No interstitials. No banner ads. Only optional "Watch Ad" rewards.
- **Non-personalized ads only.** AdMob configured for child-directed treatment (`tagForChildDirectedTreatment=true`) + non-personalized ads (`npa=true`) + `maxAdContentRating=G`.
- **No chat, no social features, no user-generated content exposed to others.**
- **No gambling mechanics.** The spin wheel awards only free in-game coins/power-ups — never real money, never random purchase outcomes.
- **No in-app purchases in v1.0.1.** The current launch build is free with zero IAP. Optional cosmetic coin packs (with a parental math gate) are planned for a later update.
- **COPPA + GDPR-K + UK AADC compliant.**
- **Offline gameplay.** Gameplay works offline — only optional rewarded video ads need a connection. **Do not claim "100% offline" or "completely offline."**

## 💰 Monetization (v1.0.1 — Transparent)

Block Kitz is **free to download and play**.

### Rewarded Ads (via Google AdMob)
- Only shown when the player taps a "Watch Ad" button (e.g., Revive after game over, or x2 Coins)
- Non-personalized only (`npa=true`)
- Kid-safe content rating (G)
- Never forced, never interrupting

### In-App Purchases
- **Not in v1.0.1 launch build.** Zero IAP currently.
- **Planned for a later update:** optional cosmetic coin packs, gated by an in-app parental math problem before the payment screen. Payments via Google Play Billing. Coins would have no real-world value.
