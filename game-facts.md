# Block Kitz — Game Facts Sheet

Use these facts verbatim or lightly rephrased in marketing copy. They are accurate as of 2026-04-22.

## 🎮 Game Overview

**Block Kitz** is a casual block-puzzle game for Android (iOS coming later). It's designed to be enjoyed by players of all ages, including children, with a strong focus on family-safe content and zero data collection.

**Tagline options:**
- "Place. Pop. Play. Block Kitz — the cozy block puzzle for everyone."
- "1,000+ levels of pure, family-safe puzzle fun."
- "No ads forced. No data collected. Just blocks."

## ✨ Core Features

### Three Distinct Game Modes

1. **Classic Mode** — An 8×8 grid where you drag & drop block pieces from a 3-piece tray onto the board. Fill full rows or columns to clear them and earn points. Features gems, obstacles, and a chapter-based journey through 10 themed worlds.

2. **Pivot Mode** — A modern Tetris-style falling-block experience. Rotate, move, and drop tetrominoes. Features SRS rotation system, T-Spin bonuses, hold piece, and guideline scoring. Progress through 100 levels.

3. **Adventure Mode** — A twist on Classic with objective-based stages: collect specific gems (stars ⭐ and pentas 🔶), defuse bomb gems, and navigate frozen cells and locked gems. 1,000+ levels with 10 visual chapters from "Hidden Glade" to "Soul's Nexus."

### What's Inside

- **1,000+ hand-crafted + procedurally-generated levels** per mode
- **10 beautifully-themed chapters** per mode (30 unique chapter backgrounds total)
- **Daily rewards** — 7-day login streak calendar with escalating prizes
- **Daily Challenge** — a fresh puzzle every day with special modifiers (Precision, Speed, Gem Rush, Ice Fury)
- **Spin wheel** — earn free spins, win coins and power-ups
- **Power-ups** — bomb, row-blast, column-blast, undo, streak shield
- **Achievements** — 20+ lifetime achievements + session-based mastery badges
- **Streaks & combos** — clear multiple lines for combo bonuses (up to 5x ULTRA COMBO)
- **Mascot reactions** — an adorable hamster cheers you on
- **Beautiful music** — 4 ambient piano moods (Peaceful Garden, Rainy Evening, Ocean Calm, Starlit Night)

## 🛡️ Family-Safe Positioning

- **No data collected.** Nothing sent off the device.
- **No forced ads.** No interstitials. No banner ads. Only optional "Watch Ad" rewards.
- **No behavioral tracking.** AdMob configured for child-directed treatment + non-personalized ads only.
- **No chat, no social features, no user-generated content exposed to others.**
- **No gambling mechanics.** The spin wheel awards only free in-game coins/power-ups — never real money, never random purchase outcomes.
- **Parental gate** on all in-app purchases (math problem required before payment).
- **COPPA + GDPR-K + UK AADC compliant.**
- **Offline playable.** No internet required.

## 💰 Monetization (Transparent)

Block Kitz is **free to download and play**. Two optional revenue streams:

### Rewarded Ads (via Google AdMob)
- Only shown when the player taps a "Watch Ad" button (e.g., Revive after game over, or x2 Coins)
- Non-personalized only
- Kid-safe content rating (G)
- Never forced, never interrupting

### In-App Purchases
- Optional **coin packs** (999 coins, 2,000 coins, 5,000 coins) — used for power-ups/boosters
- Optional **Supporter Pack** (999 coins one-time) — bonus coins, power-ups, undo charges
- All processed by Google Play Billing
- Parental math gate required before purchase flow begins
- Coins have no real-world value (game-only currency)
